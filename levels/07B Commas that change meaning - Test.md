## Question
Calgary, ___ hosted the 1988 Winter Olympics, is in Alberta.

A) that
B) which
C) who
Answer: B
Feedback A: There is only one Calgary, so the clause is extra information. A non-essential clause starts with which.
Feedback C: Who is for people, not places.
Hint: Do we already know which Calgary the sentence means?

## Question
Which sentence says that all the paralegals got a bonus?

A) The paralegals who worked late got a bonus.
B) The paralegals, who worked late, got a bonus.
Answer: B
Feedback A: With no commas, the clause picks out only the paralegals who worked late. Only some got a bonus.
Hint: Commas = all. No commas = only some.

## Question
The report, is on the desk that Omar uses. Which comma rule does this sentence break?

A) A comma separates the subject from its verb.
B) The that clause needs commas.
C) It breaks no rule.
Answer: A
Feedback B: That Omar uses tells you which desk, so it is essential and takes no commas.
Feedback C: The comma between report and is splits the subject from its verb.
Hint: Find the subject and the verb. Should anything come between them?

## Inspect: The Friday notice
Document: Memo
To: All staff
From: Dana Whitfield, Office Manager
Re: Office closure
FILLER passage. Is it ready to go out?
Passage:
The binder {that => that | It tells you which binder, so that with no commas is correct.} holds the exhibits is on my desk. Our office, {which => which | We only have one office, so which with commas is correct.} overlooks the river, will be closed on Friday. Please bring pens, sticky {notes, => notes, | The Oxford comma keeps the list clear.} and the client list on Monday.
