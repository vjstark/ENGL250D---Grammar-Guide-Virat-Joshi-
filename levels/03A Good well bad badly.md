**Rule:** After an action verb, use an adverb (*well*, *badly*). After a linking verb, use an adjective (*good*, *bad*).

✓ I feel bad about the mistake. (*feel* is a linking verb here)
✗ She did good on the exam. → well

**Be test:** replace the verb with *is* or *was*. If the sentence still makes sense, it's a linking verb, so use the adjective.
