## Question
The paralegal ___ found the error deserves credit.

A) who
B) whom
C) whomever
Answer: A
Feedback B: Rewrite the clause: he found the error. He is a subject form, so the answer is who.
Feedback C: Whomever is an object form, and it means "any person." This sentence is about one specific paralegal.
Hint: Did he or him find the error?

## Question
Give the file to ___ is at the front desk.

A) whoever
B) whomever
C) whom
Answer: A
Feedback B: The whole clause is the object of to. Inside the clause, he is at the front desk, so the pronoun is a subject.
Feedback C: Whom is an object form, but inside the clause the pronoun is the subject of is.
Hint: Ignore to. Look at the verb right after the blank.

## Question
___ do you think will win the case?

A) Who
B) Whom
C) Whomever
Answer: A
Feedback B: Cover do you think: ___ will win the case. He will win, so the answer is who.
Feedback C: Whomever is an object form, and this is a question about one person.
Hint: Cover the words do you think.
