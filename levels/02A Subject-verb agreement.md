**Rule:** The verb matches the head of the subject (its main noun), not the words around it.

✓ A shipment of shoes has been delayed. (head: shipment)
✗ The analysis of the results reveal a rise. → reveals

**Cross-out test:** cross out the phrase that starts with *of*, *for* or *with*, then read the subject and verb together.

The analysis ~~of the results~~ reveals a rise.
