# Affidavit

I, Jordan Lee, of the City of Calgary, in the Province of Alberta, make oath and say that:

1. I am the office manager of Ramsay Holdings Ltd., the Plaintiff in this action, and I have personal knowledge of the matters in this affidavit.

2. In March 2026, the Defendant delivered {less => fewer | Shipments can be counted, so use fewer.} than 12 of our 40 shipments on time.

3. The list of damaged items {are => is | The head of the subject is list, which is singular.} attached as Exhibit A.

4. The Defendant's drivers handled the boxes {bad => badly | Handled is an action verb, so it needs the adverb badly.}.

5. On April 2, 2026, the Defendant's manager sent an apology to my assistant and {I => me | To is a preposition. Drop "my assistant and": sent an apology to me.}. The manager {whom => whom | I called him, so whom is correct.} I called on April 3 did not return my call.

6. The driver {who => whom | The Defendant hired him, so the pronoun is an object: whom.} the Defendant hired in March had no training.

7. I do not know where the damaged boxes are now stored {at => | Where already means "at what place," so at adds nothing.}.

8. The invoice {which => that | The clause tells you which invoice, so use that with no commas.} the Defendant sent on April 9 is attached as Exhibit B. The Defendant has paid {less => less | Money is an amount, so less is standard.} than $500 of the $8,000 it owes.

Sworn before me at Calgary, Alberta, on April 20, 2026.
