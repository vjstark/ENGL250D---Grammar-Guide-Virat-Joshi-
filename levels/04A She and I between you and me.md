**Rule:** Use the subject form (*I, she, he, we, they*) for whoever does the action. Use the object form (*me, her, him, us, them*) for whoever receives the action, or after a preposition.

✓ She and I have been friends forever.
✗ Between you and I, the file is late. → you and me

**Drop-the-name test:** cover the other person's name. *The manager asked ~~Omar and~~ me* sounds right; *asked I* doesn't.
