**Rule:** Look only inside the word's own clause. If it acts as a subject there, use *who* or *whoever*. If it acts as an object, use *whom* or *whomever*.

✓ The client whom we called is here. (we called **him**)
✗ We will send whomever is nearest. → whoever (**he** is nearest)

**He/him swap:** rewrite the clause with *he* or *him*. *He* → who. *Him* → whom.
