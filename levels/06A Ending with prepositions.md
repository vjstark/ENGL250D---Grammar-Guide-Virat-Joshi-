**Rule:** Ending with a preposition is correct English. For very formal writing, you can move the preposition to the front. Cut a final preposition only when it adds nothing.

✓ Who should I give this to?
✗ Where is the file at? → Where is the file?

**Remove test:** take the last word away. If the meaning stays the same, cut it.
